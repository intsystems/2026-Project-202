\section{Discussion}\label{sec:cost}
\cx{In a trained generator, the closed curve traced by one neuron's delay vectors reflects periodic motion of the full network. During optimization, interpreting a change in MG requires an independent check. Freezing layers concentrates weight updates, but changes in weight-norm MG also occur in modular-addition runs whose validation accuracy stays low (\appref{app:additional}).}

\cx{A log can reveal a change even when a simpler rule suffices for a decision. In Walker2d, MG of the action norm and a fixed policy choice give nearly identical mean rewards under actuator shifts (\appref{app:rl_selection}). In the text VAE, periodic checks detect more changes at the primary budget (Table~\ref{tab:cyclic}). Recording loss accounts for most of the monitoring time (\appref{app:cost}).}
