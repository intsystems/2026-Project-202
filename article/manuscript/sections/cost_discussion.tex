\section{Discussion}\label{sec:cost}
\cx{Adding independent oscillations raises MG of a matrix norm while each entry follows one rhythm (Table~\ref{tab:matrix-observations}). In ResNet-18, update PR confirms that the fall in weight-norm MG accompanies more concentrated updates (Figure~\ref{fig:resnet-freeze-trace}). For most settings, weight-norm MG dips more deeply in grokking runs near generalization than in matched controls. Removing the local linear trend eliminates this ordering (\appref{app:grokking}).}

\cx{Encoders with higher gradient-norm MG tended to give better digit classifiers on MNIST. For forecasting, selection by MG and by validation gave similar mean errors. For classifier interventions and transformer loss spikes, changes between consecutive log values detected at least as many events as MG at comparable false-alarm rates (Table~\ref{tab:decisions}).}

\section[Conclusion]{Conclusion}
\cx{Single-neuron MG approaches one in recurrent networks that learn a periodic orbit. In a classifier, freezing the feature extractor reduces both weight-norm MG and the effective number of update directions.} Once independent measurements establish which change the log tracks, MG can help locate that change retrospectively in loss or weight-norm records from comparable runs.
