\section{Discussion and conclusion}\label{sec:cost}
\cx{A scalar log can reveal when a neural network's behavior becomes simpler. When a network learned a repeating cycle, we could see the change in the activity of just one neuron. We treated each short sequence of its readings as a point. The points traced a loop with an estimated dimension close to one. Many neurons were still active, yet one variable, the phase of the cycle, described their joint motion.}

\cx{This gives a reason to keep the history of loss or weight norm during training. Even when mean loss barely changes, the pattern of fluctuations can mark a period worth revisiting. Measurements of the weights or activations can then help explain what changed inside the network. We hope future work will show how broadly this approach applies to other kinds of scalar logs.}
}
