\section{Discussion}\label{sec:cost}
\cx{The signal we record determines which aspects of simplification MG can reveal. In the training interventions, we connect changes in MG to independently measured changes inside the network. In the grokking experiment, MG@weight norm also falls in a run that memorizes the training examples (\appref{app:additional}), illustrating why each interpretation needs independent evidence.}

\cx{Evaluating an MG-based decision requires measuring its cost and outcome. For alerts, compare detection rules on the same log at a matched false-warning rate. For forecast selection, report prediction error and the time to select and fit the model. Timing on saved logs isolates computation. In the cyclic VAE, measuring loss on fixed sentences dominates the cost of monitoring (\appref{app:cost}).}

\section[Conclusion]{\cx{Conclusion}}
\cx{We propose a common way to track dynamical simplification from a single scalar log. In our controlled recurrent systems, MG captures differences in the structure of repeated motion. In our training experiments, changes in MG from window to window track independently measured simplification, including in feedforward networks.}
