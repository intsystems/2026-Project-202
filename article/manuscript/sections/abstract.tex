\begin{abstract}
\claude{\cx{We propose a method for tracking dynamical simplification from a single scalar log. We reconstruct the dynamics from delayed samples and summarize each window with the pooled MacKay--Ghahramani (MG) estimator. Our tests span synthetic oscillators, image classification, text modeling, and reinforcement learning. MG captures differences in phase structure in controlled systems. In the learning systems we tested, MG tracks changes in dynamics that we verify with independent measurements. We also compare MG with simpler features of the same records for monitoring and model selection.}}
\end{abstract}
