\begin{abstract}
\claude{We propose a method for tracking dynamical simplification from a scalar log. The pooled MacKay--Ghahramani (MG) estimator summarizes the geometry of delayed samples in each window. We test MG on synthetic oscillators, image classifiers, text models, and reinforcement learning. MG distinguishes signals with different numbers of independent oscillations and tracks changes measured independently in our training experiments. We compare it with simpler statistics for monitoring and model selection.}
\end{abstract}
