\begin{abstract}
\claude{\cx{How can we recognize simpler dynamics from one quantity recorded over time? We represent short sequences of readings as points and estimate their local dimension in successive windows with the pooled MacKay--Ghahramani (MG) estimator. From one neuron's activation, MG distinguishes independent oscillations from harmonics. In classifier and text-model training it tracks changes verified by independent measurements. MG selects image representations close to the best tested and forecasters with errors close to those of held-out model search. Simple differences between successive readings match or improve on its detection of classifier interventions and transformer loss spikes.}}
\end{abstract}
