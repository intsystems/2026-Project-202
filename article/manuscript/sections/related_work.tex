\subsection[Related work]{\cx{Related work}}\label{sec:related-work}
\cx{Delay coordinates can reconstruct a deterministic system's state from a suitable scalar observation \citep{takens1981detecting,sauer1991embedology}. \citet{grassberger1983measuring} estimated correlation dimension by counting nearby pairs of reconstructed points. We use the nearest-neighbor estimator of \citet{levina2004maximum} with the pooling of \citet{mackay2005comments}. Its interpretation relies on repeated visits and preservation of local geometry (\appref{app:theory}).}

\cx{Generalization has been studied through the dimension of weight trajectories \citep{simsekli2020hausdorff,birdal2021intrinsic}. \citet{redman2024equivalent} compare training time scales using delayed measurements of selected weights. Our coordinates are samples of a single quantity, such as the norm of all the weights.}

\cx{\citet{notsawo2024predicting} used early loss oscillations to anticipate delayed generalization. We test retrospectively whether weight-norm MG dips near the observed rise in accuracy (\appref{app:grokking}). RankMe selects encoders from the effective rank of image features \citep{garrido2023rankme}. We compare it with selection from gradient-norm histories on the same classification task (Section~\ref{sec:ssl}).}
}
