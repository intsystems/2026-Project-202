Effective rank can fall while active dimension stays fixed. The scalar drop de- scribes a transition retrospectively; it does not establish early prediction. • Stochastic: noise shifts the value and makes it depend on embedding settings. • Transient: temporal exclusion can produce a large spurious value. Only one learned-system benchmark passes every prescribed check. All validation systems are externally forced. 06 Estimation limits and experimental confounders • Recovery depends on the lag, observer and window. The tested resolution ceiling is low. • One memorising run falls just as deeply elsewhere. Depth alone does not distinguish generalisation. • The grokking comparison has four regularised runs against two without weight decay. Regularisation and outcome are confounded. • The scalar effect disappears after linear detrending; roughness changes more strongly. The trajectory result does not reproduce in the full-batch setting. Interpret the geometry only after checking the regime. For these grokking runs, the evidence supports a reversible redistribution of trajectory variance.
