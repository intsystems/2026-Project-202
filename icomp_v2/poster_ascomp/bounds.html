Only one learned-system benchmark passes every prescribed check. All validation systems are externally forced. 06 Scope of the evidence • Recovery depends on the lag, observer and window. The tested resolution ceiling is low. • One memorising run falls just as deeply elsewhere. Depth alone does not distinguish generalisation. • The grokking comparison has four regularised runs against two without weight decay. Regularisation and outcome are confounded. • The scalar effect disappears after linear detrending; roughness changes more strongly. The trajectory result does not reproduce in the full-batch setting. Interpret the geometry only after checking the regime. For these grokking runs, the evidence supports a reversible redistribution of trajectory variance.
