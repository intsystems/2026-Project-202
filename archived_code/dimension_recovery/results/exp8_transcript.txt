3  Retraction: the 'rise, not fall' separation does not survive
===============================================================================
  report_0808.md section 6.3 reported that generalising runs excurse to
  3.2-18.0 while controls stay below 2.37. Two things were wrong with it.

  (a) The statistic was contaminated. exp7_broader.py measured the maximum
      over everything after t_mem WITHOUT capping at t_gen, so for a
      generalising run it included post-generalisation windows, where the
      weight norm flattens and the estimate degenerates towards max_E. The
      12-18 values were that artefact, not a rise before the transition.

  (b) Capped correctly at t_gen, and scored on runs it was not built on, the
      separation is gone:
