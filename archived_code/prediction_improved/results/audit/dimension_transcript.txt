C  (E0.1b)  A system whose dimension changes 2 -> 1 at a step we know
===============================================================================
  A 2-torus whose second amplitude is ramped to zero over steps 8000-12000, so
  the true dimension is 2 before and 1 after. 2000 samples, published trace
  settings. If the estimator cannot resolve this, it cannot resolve anything
  weaker, and every claim in the paper about a dimension change is bounded by
  this result.

   cycles/win    W  d before  d after   ratio   true   verdict
          5.7    0      3.19     1.44    2.21    2.0   resolves it
          5.7   14      5.68     1.44    3.94    2.0   overshoots
         20.3    0      3.39     1.20    2.82    2.0   resolves it
         20.3   14      3.38     1.20    2.82    2.0   resolves it
         50.9    0      2.93     1.44    2.04    2.0   resolves it
         50.9   14      2.93     1.44    2.04    2.0   resolves it

  The true ratio is 2.0 in every row, and the estimator finds it. This is a
  POSITIVE result and it must be reported as one: the machinery is not broken,
  and a real 2 -> 1 collapse in a well-sampled window is recovered to within
  the calibration error of section A. What section B establishes is the
  precondition -- several returns inside the window -- and what section D
  establishes is that the training logs do not meet it. The failure is
  specific and diagnosable, not a general objection to the method.

E  (E1.1b)  The same observable through a smooth monotone reparameterisation
===============================================================================
  h(x) for h a diffeomorphism of the data range. Takens/Stark say nothing
  changes: h o phi is as good an observable as phi, the images are
  diffeomorphic, the dimension is identical. This is the single cheapest test
  of whether the number is geometry or arithmetic.

  The first four warps are smooth but nearly affine ACROSS ONE WINDOW, because
  a window spans a small part of the run's range. Those are the weak half of
  the test. The last two are diffeomorphisms of R whose curvature is tuned to
  the within-window range itself, so they cannot be absorbed into a local
  affine map. Those are the sharp half.

  invariant for the SET but the estimator sees 300 points at a fixed scale.

===============================================================================

  null that matters keeps the local trend and destroys everything else: fit a
  quadratic, phase-randomise the residual, recombine. If the observed estimate
